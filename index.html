DOC · HOW A JOB RUNS

Drawing to dispatch.

Every job follows the same six steps, whether it is one prototype or a production batch of ten thousand.

  1. 01

    Drawing received

    Send a 2D drawing or 3D model. Features, tolerances and GD&T are read before anything is priced.

  2. 02

    Engineering review

    Feasibility and manufacturability issues are flagged back to you before machining starts, not after.

  3. 03

    Quotation

    An itemised quote covering material, machining and finishing, with no costs added later.

    Within 24 hrs
  4. 04

    Programming & setup

    Toolpaths and fixturing are planned around your drawing, rather than forced into a standard process.

  5. 05

    Machining

    VMC job work, CNC milling and surface grinding, run in whatever sequence the part actually needs.

    2–15 days
  6. 06

    Inspection & dispatch

    Every batch is checked against the critical dimensions on your drawing before it is packed and dispatched.
