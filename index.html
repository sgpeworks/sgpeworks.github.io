SEQ · JOB FLOW

From your drawing to your dock, in nine steps.

Every job — prototype or production — moves through the same sequence, so quality and timelines stay predictable.

1

Customer sends drawing

Share PDF, DWG, DXF, STEP or IGES files along with quantity and material through our quote form, WhatsApp or email.

2

Engineering review

Our team studies tolerances, GD&T callouts and finish requirements to plan the right process and fixturing.

3

Quotation within 24 hours

You receive a costed, itemised quotation covering material, machining and finish — usually within one business day.

4

Material received

Material is received from you or procured on your behalf and checked against the drawing's specification before machining.

5

Machining

Parts are cut on VMC and CNC equipment against your drawing, with in-process checks at each operation.

6

Quality inspection

Finished components are measured against critical dimensions and tolerances before they're approved for dispatch.

7

Packing

Parts are packed to protect finish and precision surfaces in transit, labelled against your purchase order.

8

Dispatch

Consignments are handed to our logistics partners with tracking shared directly with you.

9

Customer receives finished parts

Parts land at your dock ready for assembly or inspection, with support if any query comes up after delivery.
