Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

SG Precision Engineering ("we", "us", "our") operates this website and provides VMC job work, CNC milling and related precision machining services. This policy explains what information we collect through the website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

When you submit a quote request, contact form or drawing upload, we collect the details you provide, which may include:

  • Name, company name and job title
  • Phone number and email address
  • GST number, city and other business details you choose to share
  • Drawings and files you upload (PDF, DWG, DXF, STEP, IGES, ZIP)
  • Any message or job description you enter

We also collect standard technical information automatically, such as browser type, device type and pages visited, through normal website operation.

2. How we use your information

  • To review your drawing and prepare a quotation
  • To respond to enquiries by phone, WhatsApp or email
  • To process and fulfil confirmed job orders
  • To maintain records required for GST and business compliance
  • To improve our website and services

We do not sell your information to third parties. We do not use your drawings or technical files for any purpose other than quoting and completing your job.

3. How we share information

Quote and contact form submissions are delivered to our team via a third-party form-processing service (FormSubmit) and email. We may share limited information with logistics partners solely to dispatch finished parts to you.

4. Data retention

We retain enquiry details and job records for as long as needed for business, tax and legal compliance purposes, after which they are deleted or archived securely.

5. Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete personal information you have submitted, subject to any records we are legally required to keep, by emailing sgpeworks@gmail.com.

6. Cookies

This website uses only the technical cookies or local storage necessary for it to function correctly. It does not run third-party advertising trackers.

7. Contact us

For any privacy-related question, write to us at sgpeworks@gmail.com or call +91 80105 12390.
